# Corporate compliance learning

A practical learning project for a technical founder exploring a startup that automates corporate compliance.

The approach follows the Pareto principle: prioritize the concepts and workflows that explain a large part of the field, then specialize after identifying a customer and problem. The goal is working literacy for discovery and product decisions.

## Start here

1. Read the [six-week learning plan](learning-plan.md).
2. Open the [Week 1 chapter as a PDF](chapters/week-01/Week_1_Compliance_Foundations_EN_HU.pdf), or [read it on GitHub](chapters/week-01/chapter.md).
3. Complete the company-mapping exercise and discuss a recent workflow with a practitioner.

The Week 1 PDF has 28 pages, a clickable chapter map, bookmarks, an English-Hungarian glossary, worked examples, exercises, explained answers, and linked original readings.

## Learning approach

- **Broad corporate scope.** Cover governance, risk, compliance, controls, assurance, conduct, financial crime, reporting, privacy, security, resilience, and relevant sector specialties.
- **Plain English with Hungarian support.** Define jargon when it first appears. Add useful Hungarian equivalents such as governance (*vállalatirányítás*), compliance (*megfelelőség*), and assurance (*bizonyosságot nyújtó értékelés*).
- **Examples before abstraction.** Connect each important idea to a business decision, person, action, and supporting record.
- **Self-contained chapters.** Explain the material in full. Original readings provide context and deeper study.
- **Practical application.** Include worked cases, a small exercise, and explanations of the answers.
- **Founder perspective.** Identify the user, decision-maker, reviewer, buyer, data, workflow, and measurable value of potential automation.
- **Source-grounded learning.** Fetch and read authoritative material. Preserve source names, editions, dates, scope, and useful page references.

The organizing question is: **Which requirement applies, who must act, and how would we know that it happened?**

## Roadmap

Plan for five hours per week, for approximately 30 hours total. Material availability does not imply that the learner has completed the week.

## Working on future chapters

Read [agents.md](agents.md) before editing or creating material. Keep the plain-language, example-driven, English-with-Hungarian approach. Preserve the broad learning objective and create only the requested material.

Use primary sources and distinguish applicable legal requirements, source interpretations, internal policies, fictional scenarios, and product hypotheses. DunaWorks Group is the fictional company used in Week 1; its thresholds and time limits are teaching examples.

Maintain both the chapter text and its PDF when updating a chapter. Check links and inspect the rendered pages before marking an edition ready.
